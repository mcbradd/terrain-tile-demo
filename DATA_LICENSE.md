# Data and asset licenses

## Elevation data

`data/source/` holds a crop of the USGS 3D Elevation Program (3DEP) 1/3 arc-second DEM.

Credit: Elevation: U.S. Geological Survey, 3D Elevation Program, 1/3 arc-second DEM. Public domain.

All 3DEP products are in the public domain. Source URLs, retrieval time, and checksums for each crop are recorded in the `.json` file next to it.
